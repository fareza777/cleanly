Cleanly

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Version 1.5.2

Cleanly is an offline-first cleaning checklist and timer. The cleaning sessions, routines, schedules, and history you create are stored on your device. Cleanly does not require an account and does not operate a developer-managed cloud or analytics backend.

Information stored by Cleanly

Cleanly stores the information you enter or create in the app, such as selected rooms, checklist progress, cleaning routines, schedules, reminder preferences, theme preferences, and cleaning history. This information is stored locally in the app on your device and is used to provide the app's features.

Cleanly does not intentionally collect names, contact details, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, or payment details. The app does not read your files or contacts.

Notifications

If you create and enable a schedule, Cleanly may request Android notification permission and schedule local reminders on your device. Reminder content is generated from your local schedule. You can turn notifications off in Android settings or inside Cleanly.

Advertising and consent

Cleanly uses Google Mobile Ads to display a banner in selected browse screens, an occasional interstitial after a completed cleaning session, and an optional rewarded ad when you explicitly choose to unlock an in-app theme or extended cleaning preset. An ad is never shown during an active cleaning timer.

Google Mobile Ads and Google's consent technology may process device, app, and advertising information to deliver, measure, limit, or personalize ads where permitted. The choices available to you depend on your region. Cleanly requests consent through Google's User Messaging Platform when required and provides Ad privacy options in Settings when available. More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your Cleanly data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Cleanly. Cleanly does not provide automatic cloud backup. Android backup is disabled for this app. Advertising providers may retain their own information according to their policies and controls.

Children

Cleanly is a general household productivity app and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent. Android system services may be used for local notifications. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Cleanly's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated with any revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the Cleanly GitHub repository. Please do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
