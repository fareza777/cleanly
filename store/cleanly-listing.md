### Short description

Simple cleaning checklists and timers for the time you actually have.

### Full description

Cleaning the whole home can feel like a lot. Cleanly turns the next few minutes into a clear, doable plan.

Choose the time you have — 5, 10, 20, or 30 minutes — pick a room, and follow a checklist made for that session. Cleanly keeps the focus on the next useful step, not on an endless list of chores.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Start a timed cleaning session for the kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, living room, or a quick home reset.
• Get a checklist that adapts to the time you choose.
• See the next task and its quick instruction while the timer runs.
• Tick off steps, pause when life happens, add another step, or finish early.
• Save custom cleaning routines for the jobs you repeat.
• Set daily or weekly local reminders for a room, duration, and time that fit your rhythm.
• Look back at your cleaning history, weekly minutes, room totals, current streak, and best streak.
• Use dark mode and reduced motion when you prefer a calmer interface.
• Keep your core cleaning tools available offline, without an account or cloud sync.

SMALL CLEANS COUNT

Cleanly is made for real homes and real schedules. Five focused minutes still moves something forward. A short session is a completed session, and every little win gives you a clearer next step.

HOW CLEANLY USES ADS

Cleanly may show a banner in selected browse screens, an occasional interstitial after a completed session, and an optional rewarded ad when you explicitly choose to unlock an in-app theme or extended cleaning preset. Ads do not appear during an active cleaning timer. You can review available ad privacy choices from Settings.

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Cleaning sessions, routines, schedules, preferences, and history are stored locally on your device. Cleanly has no account and no developer-managed cloud sync. See the privacy policy for information about local storage, notifications, consent, and Google Mobile Ads.
